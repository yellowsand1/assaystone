# v0.30.0 — Say what blocks the next stage, and hand over the patch

Released 2026-11-25.

`capability status` says what is unresolved. That is a report, not a next action.
`capability next` says what blocks the promotion, and gives you the JSON that
removes it.

## Highlights

- **`capability next`** reports, in order: a missing stage policy (with the
  policy to add), a stage that permits nothing (with the approval oracle and the
  flags), a critical criterion the stage does not gate, and — when a trail is
  supplied — a required oracle with no passing decision on that trail.
- **The patch is part of the output.** A draft the owner still reviews and signs,
  but nobody has to guess the shape of an approval oracle.
- **The trial ends with it.** `docs/trial.md` step 7 is now the next action
  rather than a residual-risk list, and the document is still regenerated and
  diffed in CI.

## Evidence

- 662 tests, lint clean, CI on Python 3.10–3.13, seven required checks.
- `python scripts/trial.py --markdown | diff - docs/trial.md` passes.

## Known limits

- The planner knows the manifest and the trail. It does not know why you want to
  promote, and it cannot tell whether an approval is a good one.
- A missing stage policy produces a template; the requirements in it are the
  oracles the manifest already has, not a considered policy.
- The semantic and organisational limits in `docs/plane.md` are unchanged.
